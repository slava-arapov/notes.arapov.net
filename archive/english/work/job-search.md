# Job Search

Take some time to reflect before looking for a new job. It helps you take stock of your experience, strengths, and weaknesses, prepare for behavioral interviews, and come up with questions for the interviewer.

In [How do you interview?](https://vas3k.club/post/15768/), Egor Biriukov suggests writing a candid essay about your recent experience. The exercise can help you identify:

- Success stories about a problem you noticed and fixed
- Stories about things that went poorly, what you learned, and what you did to keep them from happening again
- Things you liked and disliked, which can become questions for the team or hiring manager

Then work through the following steps.

1. Research the market to see what skills and technologies companies look for.
1. Make a checklist of technologies and work on the gaps.
1. [Update your résumé](/work/cv.md) and LinkedIn profile.
1. Tidy up your GitHub profile. Add an introduction and give each project a README that explains what it does, what it uses, and how to run it.
1. [Prepare for interviews](/work/interview/).
1. Refresh your profiles on LinkedIn and job boards.
1. Send out your résumé, ideally with a cover letter.
   - Apply through job boards.
   - Contact companies you find interesting.
   - Find employers by searching Google for `remote jobs` or `remote positions`.
   - Contact recruiters you meet in chat groups.
