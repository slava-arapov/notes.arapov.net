# Job Interviews

Interviewing well and doing the job well are different skills. Interviewing takes practice of its own.

Reasons to interview include:

- Earning more, since hiring budgets can be bigger than retention budgets
- Learning about industry trends and how companies work
- Practicing communication
- Building a professional network
- Feeling more confident with an offer in hand

Interview formats vary, but companies often borrow stages from each other. A typical process includes:

- An initial conversation with a recruiter to check basic fit
- Short technical questions
- Coding
- System design
- Behavioral questions

Companies often combine several stages in one interview. If part of the interview may be in English, prepare a short introduction in English.

::: tip
- Go into an interview rested, well, and after a good night’s sleep.
- Avoid early mornings and evenings.
- Schedule up to two interviews a day.
- Interview with at most three companies at once.
- Keep your résumé where you can see it.
:::

::: tip
Talk to a recruiter before you start interviewing. A consultation can help you get feedback on your résumé, practice answering questions, and decide what kind of work to look for.

The original note mentions [H](https://h.careers) and quotes four consultations at RUB 4,500 per session.
:::

## What to ask the company

If you are applying for remote work paid in a foreign currency, ask early whether US or EU citizenship is required.

Record the answers in a spreadsheet so you can compare offers.

- Office locations, including international offices
- Company size in Russia and worldwide
- Type of company
- Project and technology stack
- Role on the project and what the ideal candidate looks like
- Expected tasks
- Team size and roles, such as developers and testers
- Take-home pay
- Contract terms
- Vacation and days off
- Benefits
- Commute time by public transport
- Workstation
- Training and professional development
- Career progression
- Working processes
- Firewall restrictions
- Whether you will communicate in English
- Schedule flexibility and time tracking
- Your rating of the office on a scale of 1 to 10
- Overall impression

Two more questions worth asking:

- What would my first task be?
- Imagine you hired someone a year ago and they turned out to be your ideal hire. What did they do over the year to earn that assessment?
