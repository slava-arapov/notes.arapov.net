# Coding Interviews

## Algorithms round

The algorithms or whiteboard round varies in difficulty by company. It is relatively easy to prepare for because many problems use the same patterns.

Practice choosing an approach and solving the problem. Both skills take a lot of practice. During the interview, show how you reason, how you choose a solution, and how you test it.

### Preparation

[Egor Biriukov suggests](https://vas3k.club/post/15768/#Whiteboard-algoritmy) the following plan.

- Take an algorithms course, such as the [Data Structures and Algorithms Specialization](https://ru.coursera.org/specializations/data-structures-algorithms).
- Work through one of these books:
  - [Grokking Algorithms](https://www.ozon.ru/product/grokaem-algoritmy-illyustrirovannoe-posobie-dlya-programmistov-i-lyubopytstvuyushchih-139296295/?sh=v_JSM2C-rg)
  - [Algorithms (Dasgupta, Vazirani)](http://algorithmics.lsi.upc.edu/docs/Dasgupta-Papadimitriou-Vazirani.pdf)
  - [Algorithm Design (Kleinberg, Tardos)](https://ict.iitk.ac.in/wp-content/uploads/CS345-Algorithms-II-Algorithm-Design-by-Jon-Kleinberg-Eva-Tardos.pdf)
- Skim the older book [Cracking the Coding Interview](https://www.amazon.com/Cracking-Coding-Interview-Programming-Questions/dp/0984782850).
- Solve problems on LeetCode or similar sites until you can spot patterns and identify different problem types. Try working through [a set of problems](https://leetcode.com/discuss/general-discussion/460599/blind-75-leetcode-questions).
- Do mock interviews with people you know or on [interviewing.io](https://interviewing.io/), [Pramp](https://www.pramp.com/), or [CodeSignal](https://codesignal.com/).

### During the interview

- Say hello and make conversation.
- Listen to the problem.
- Clarify the input format and size, time and memory limits, who will use the function, and whether preprocessing is allowed. Ask about anything you would otherwise have to assume.
- Compare a brute-force solution with a more efficient one.
- Talk through the algorithm.
- Write clean code.
- Think aloud as you code, including alternatives and complexity.
- Estimate the complexity.
- Discuss alternatives and their trade-offs.

::: tip
Keep talking even if you are unsure where to start. Propose a rough solution and explain why you would rule it out.
:::
