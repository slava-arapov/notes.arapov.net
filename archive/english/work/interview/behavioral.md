# Behavioral Interviews

The interviewer wants to understand how you handle different situations, what you are like to work with, and how you use your interpersonal skills.

Common questions cover career preferences and plans, experience and projects on your résumé, and specific examples that start with “Tell me about a time when…”

Interviewers often look for:

- Empathy
- Initiative and willingness to take on responsibility
- Project management skills
- How you handle conflict

Give the hiring manager concrete examples they can use to assess you against their interview criteria.

## Preparation

1. Learn the STAR method.
1. Gather examples of things that went well and things that went poorly.
1. Find common questions, use the [Amazon Leadership Principles](https://www.amazon.jobs/en-gb/principles), or look up the company’s competency framework.
1. Match each competency with a story.
1. Rehearse in front of a mirror or in a mock interview.
1. Prepare [questions to ask the employer](https://vas3k.club/post/11645/).

::: tip
Answer honestly so you have a better chance of finding a role you will enjoy.
:::

## Answering questions

- Start telling the story without asking whether it counts.
- If you do not have an exact match, share a similar example.
- Keep the answer brief and leave out unnecessary details.
- Use all four parts of STAR: situation, task, action, and result.
