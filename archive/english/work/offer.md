# Job Offers and Salary

Aim to finish the interview process with several comparable offers so you have room to negotiate.

For junior and mid-level developers, experience, challenging projects, the team, and interesting work are usually the priorities.

## Salaries

To get a sense of the market:

1. Discuss salaries with colleagues, friends, and acquaintances
1. Look at salary surveys
1. Check salary expectations in [other candidates’ résumés on HeadHunter](https://hh.ru/employer)
1. Check salary expectations of [employees at companies you are interested in on Habr Career](https://career.habr.com/resumes?company_ids%5B%5D=1000063412)

These were rough figures for the Russian market in spring 2022.

- Junior — under RUB 100,000
- Mid-level — RUB 120,000–300,000
- Senior — around RUB 400,000
