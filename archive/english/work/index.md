# Work

In IT, it pays to stay up to date with industry trends and salaries.

Regularly review your job and projects against your goals, how much you enjoy the work, and what you earn. Think about where you are now, where you want to be in a year, and where you want to be in five years.

You can improve your situation within your current company or move to a different one. Earlier in an IT career, [changing jobs](/work/job-search.md) regularly can help you take on more complex work and increase your pay faster.

There are several ways to make a living.

- Employment
- Running a business
- Freelancing
- Blogging
- Teaching

Ways to increase your salary include:

- Move to a different country, region, or city
- Switch technology stacks or programming languages
- Change companies
- Build deeper knowledge of your domain
- Improve your language skills
- Work more efficiently
- Spend enough time at one company to know its product well
- Develop rare, specialized skills
- Learn to manage people
- Improve your negotiation skills
