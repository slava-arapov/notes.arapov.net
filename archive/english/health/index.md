# Health

Taking care of my lifestyle improves my chances of living a long and happy life.

I try to stick to these habits and return to the checklist as soon as possible when I fall out of my routine.

## Healthy lifestyle checklist

### Nutrition

- [ ] Eat a varied diet in moderation
- [ ] Avoid overeating
- [ ] Eat plenty of fresh vegetables and fruit

### Physical activity

- [ ] Do morning exercises
- [ ] Do basic exercises for my core, legs, posture, and neck
- [ ] Go for walks

### Other habits

- [ ] Get enough sleep
- [ ] Pay attention to my body position and posture
- [ ] Keep toilet visits short (3–4 minutes)
- [ ] Avoid heavy lifting
- [ ] Engage my pelvic floor muscles when needed, such as when changing positions, lifting, or exercising
