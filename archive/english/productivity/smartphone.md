# Smartphone

Smartphones are designed to capture our attention and keep us coming back. Mobile apps use subtle techniques that can be hard to resist.

A few ideas for taking back control:

- Turn off notifications that do not come from people
- Switch to grayscale so bright colors do not grab your attention
- Keep only useful apps on the home screen
- Be careful with apps that have an infinite feed
- Move sources out of social media and into RSS, email newsletters, or Telegram where possible
